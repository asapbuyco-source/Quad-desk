1. NEW PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ORDER FLOW                                    (Sidebar > Derivatives · "Order Flow")
  The execution-quality page. Implements the reviewer's priority order:
  classification -> synchronized venue data -> rolling flow -> price impact
  -> liquidity events.

  What's on it:
    • Impact band — Spot delta 15s, Perp delta 15s, Signed flow 15s,
      Perp price change 15s.
    • Price-impact diagnostics — I = dP / V(signed) per venue, with a
      "net flow ~ 0" guard and a 60s baseline comparison. Flags possible
      seller/buyer absorption and breakout/breakdown development.
    • Rolling flow matrix — 1s / 5s / 15s / 60s, buy / sell / net / print count,
      SPOT and PERP shown separately.
    • Executed trades — newest first: millisecond time, price, size, AGGRESSOR
      (taker side) and MARKET (SPOT / PERP). Whale prints highlighted.
    • Liquidity events — event-level book changes: EXECUTED (size removed with a
      matching print), CANCELLED (removed without one), REPLENISHED (size added),
      tagged by venue and by Binance update id (u).

  Data sources: Binance spot + USDM perp WebSockets (aggTrade, diff-depth),
  plus a REST snapshot per venue for book synchronization.

FUNDING & OI                                  (Sidebar > Derivatives · "Funding & OI")
    • Cross-venue table — mark price, funding and open interest for
      Binance / OKX / Bybit side by side.
    • Coinbase premium — US spot vs global price (US bid pressure proxy).
    • Funding heatmap — most positive / most negative funding across all
      Binance USDM perpetuals.
    • Open-interest leaderboard — OI in USD with 24h OI change and 24h price
      change (rising OI + rising price = new longs).

MACRO & FX                                    (Sidebar > Research · "Macro & FX")
    • Rates & inflation grid from FRED — Fed Funds, US 10Y, US 2Y, 10Y-2Y
      spread, CPI (YoY), unemployment, dollar index, VIX. Each card shows its
      own as-of date and series ID.
    • FX strip — ECB reference rates via Frankfurter.
    • A short "how to read this" guide.

DESK                                          (Sidebar > Overview · "Desk")
    • Fixed the overlapping-panels bug: the metrics band was expanding past its
      grid row and covering the book/tape/sentinel. Rows are now
      auto + minmax(0,1fr) with min-h-0 everywhere.
    • Order-flow metrics condensed into a single responsive band of 5 cards.
    • Mobile: an Order Book / Trade Tape switcher appears below md; desktop
      keeps both side by side.

SENTINEL                                      (Panel on the Desk, right column)
    • Everything behind each signal is visible in the detail modal: formula,
      live variables, logic gates — and now INVALIDATION CONDITIONS.
    • Invalidation is auto-evaluated. A red "VOID" chip appears on the row and
      the modal shows "Active — signal void" when the void condition holds:
          Z-Score    void when |z| < 1.5
          Bayesian   void when regime != TRENDING or posterior < 0.4
          RSI        void in the 40-60 neutral band
          Skew       void when |gamma| < 0.5
          E[X]       void when R:R < 1.5
