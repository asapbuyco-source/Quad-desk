WHAT THE PDF WAS
  An 8-page review of the order-flow / tape / depth (pages 1 and 6-8 were blank
  or images; the substance was pages 2-5). It is a trading-microstructure
  critique with a prescribed build order, not a generic UI review.

  Its thesis: the tape and depth show PRICES and SIZES but not WHO was
  aggressive or WHAT happened to resting liquidity — so you cannot distinguish
  absorption from genuine progress.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIVE SECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(§2) WHAT IS MISSING FROM THE CURRENT TAPE
    Keep:   raw trade prints (time, price, direction, size).
    Improve aggressor classification — the green up / red down arrows must mean
            BUYER-INITIATED / SELLER-INITIATED (taker side), not simply price
            ticked up or down. Confirm from the data source.
    Tag:    every trade with exchange, instrument, execution timestamp, price,
            quantity and aggressor side.
    Add:    rolling flow totals over 1s / 5s / 15s / 60s — buy volume, sell
            volume, net delta and trade count. Keep individual prints available.
    Improve depth changes — the Depth Engine shows levels and sizes but not
            whether size was EXECUTED, CANCELLED or REPLENISHED. Need
            event-by-event changes in resting liquidity, synchronized with trades.

(§3) THE LAYOUT THEY WOULD BUILD
    "BTC Quant Execution" (illustrative):
      - Header metrics: SPOT DELTA · 15s, PERP DELTA · 15s, PRICE VS LEVEL.
      - A. Executed trades, newest first:
             TIME | PRICE | SIZE | AGGRESSOR | MARKET (Spot / Perp)
      - B. Flow impact: SIGNED FLOW · 15s, PRICE CHANGE · 15s, plus a diagnostic
             (e.g. "possible buy-side absorption": buying is strong but price
             progress is small — check whether asks are being executed AND
             replenished before concluding sellers are absorbing buyers).
      - C. Liquidity event log: EVENT | OBSERVATION, e.g.
             Ask depth declines after executions
             Replenishment — new ask size appears at the same level
             Price response — price repeatedly fails to advance
    (The figures in the PDF are explicitly hypothetical, for layout only.)

(§4) THE MOST IMPORTANT METRIC TO ADD
    Price impact per unit of signed aggressive volume, measured SEPARATELY for
    spot and perpetuals:

        I_t = dP_t / V(signed)_t

    At supply:
      - aggressive buying rises but price impact stays LOW   -> possible SELLER absorption
      - aggressive buying rises and impact INCREASES         -> buyers making progress, breakout may develop
      - aggressive buying FADES and price falls              -> demand may be exhausted
      - aggressive selling rises but price REFUSES to fall   -> possible BUYER absorption

    Caveat: do NOT use the ratio blindly when net delta is near zero. Compare
    against recent baselines and volatility, and check the order book for
    replenishment and cancellations.

(§5) CHANGES TO THE EXISTING DASHBOARD
    Cumulative Delta   keep; show reset point, unit, rolling-window delta
    Regime             keep as context, NOT as an entry signal
    Book Imbalance     keep; expose the depth range and the exact formula
    Cumulative OFI     keep only if computed from actual book updates, clearly defined
    MTF OFI            keep only if each timeframe uses synchronized, comparable data
    Depth Engine       add executed / cancelled / replenished size where data supports it
    Trade Tape         add exchange, spot/perp identity, aggressor classification,
                       rolling aggregation
    Sentinel System    show the underlying evidence AND invalidation conditions
                       behind every signal

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENT
    Synchronize the trade stream and the order-book stream by EXCHANGE
    TIMESTAMPS and SEQUENCE NUMBERS. Otherwise a price response can be
    mis-attributed to the wrong trades or liquidity event.

PRIORITY ORDER
    correct trade classification -> synchronized event data -> rolling
    spot/perp flow -> price impact -> liquidity replenishment/cancellation
    analysis.

WHAT WE DID NOT TAKE (and why)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Full cross-stream sequence joining
        We correlate trades to liquidity events by TIME PROXIMITY (~2.5s), not a
        shared sequence id. Binance does not share a sequence id between the
        trade and depth streams, so an exact join needs a different mechanism.

    MTF OFI "synchronized, comparable data"
        Left as-is; its windows are OFI-history based, not cross-venue synchronized.

    "Regime as context, not an entry signal"
        That is a bot/strategy change. It was handled on the bot side
        (TREND-only entries after the 5-year backtest work), not in the frontend.
