Main edge leaks found
---------------------
1. CVD divergence confidence semantics are wrong for short signals.

In bot\main.py, _apply_cvd_divergence_gate adjusts metrics["bayesianPosterior"] before _bayesian_fusion.
But bayesianPosterior is used as P(bull). For SELL signals, _bayesian_fusion inverts it as 1 - P(bull).

This means an opposing bullish divergence can lower P(bull), then the SELL fusion can become stronger after inversion.

Example:
- BTC loser at 00:57:
  - Opposing BULLISH divergence, strength=0.789, confirms=2.
  - CVDGate: 33.92% -> 29.42%.
  - BayesFusion SELL then became 73.35%.
  - The trade entered short and lost.

This is the biggest strategy bug found in the audit.

2. Opposing CVD divergence can be overpowered by later boosts.

Example:
- ETH loser at 04:25:
  - BULLISH CVD divergence, strength=0.513, confirms=3, vol_spike=2.37x.
  - Bot still entered SELL after VPOC and Bayes/ULIS stack.
  - Trade stopped out for -2.05.

Opposing divergence should cap or veto final confidence, not merely subtract a few percent before other boosts.

3. Short entries are allowed too late into downside extension.

Example:
- SOL short at 00:54:
  - RSI=28.0, Z=-0.91, OFI=+0.1, but bot entered SELL due to bearish CVD/ULIS.
  - This is late-short risk. In volatile markets, continuation shorts need stronger fresh confirmation when RSI is already oversold.

4. Absolute/session CVD can dominate fresh flow.

Trend logic uses CVD level, OFI, tape, Bayes, and Z. A large accumulated CVD value can keep bias alive even when fresh OFI/tape are weakening.
The strategy should use fresh CVD delta and divergence state more strongly than absolute session CVD.

5. Partial profits exist in code but are too far away for current behavior.

The executor has partial profit logic, but no partials occurred in the logs.
This is not because partial execution is missing now; it is because the configured partial trigger was never reached.

6. Time exits are doing too much work.

The bot frequently exits by regime time or hard max time instead of SL/TP or designed partial/runners.
This suggests the entry horizon, SL distance, target RR, and hold timer are not calibrated to the same market behavior.

7. GEX sweep logic is probably not real yet.

Because gamma is summed without sign, GEX is biased toward positive/pin behavior. Negative gamma sweep zones need signed dealer exposure or the feature should be downgraded to informational only.

Highest-value improvements
--------------------------
P0 - Must fix before scaling size:

1. Fix CVD divergence/Bayes semantics.
- Do not overwrite bayesianPosterior with direction-adjusted confidence.
- Store CVD divergence as explicit directional features, for example:
  - cvd_divergence_direction
  - cvd_divergence_strength
  - cvd_opposes_signal
  - cvd_signal_cap
- Apply penalty/cap after directional confidence is computed.
- Suggested rule:
  - Opposing divergence strength >= 0.50 with confirms >= 2 caps final confidence below entry threshold.
  - Opposing divergence strength >= 0.75 vetoes, even without volume spike.
  - Confirming divergence can boost, but only if fresh OFI/tape are not opposite.

2. Add continuation-quality gates for trend entries.
- For SELL in VOLATILE/TREND:
  - Avoid shorting RSI < 30 unless there is real breakout continuation:
    z_ret aligned, OFI <= -0.15, tape SELL/SCREAMING, and structure break.
  - Require fresh CVD delta, not just negative accumulated CVD.
- Mirror this for LONG when RSI > 70.

3. Recalibrate exits around observed MFE.
- Current sample never reached 0.50R.
- Test partials at 0.25R, 0.33R, and 0.40R.
- Keep a runner only when the trade has strong continuation evidence.
- If the bot is meant to capture 2R+ moves, then hold-time and trend confirmation need to be much stronger.

4. Add runtime cross-symbol exposure control.
- BTC/ETH/SOL should not all open same-direction crypto beta exposure without a portfolio risk budget.
- Example:
  - max 1 full-risk same-direction trade at a time
  - second correlated trade at half risk only if confidence is higher
  - third correlated trade blocked

P1 - High priority:

5. Build per-symbol calibration.
- BTC, ETH, and SOL are behaving differently:
  - BTC is trading but slightly losing.
  - ETH has many Bayes/confidence/HTF blocks but still had the largest loss.
  - SOL is throughput-blocked heavily and slightly positive gross.
- Calibrate Bayes floor, HTF strictness, throughput baseline, partial R, and time exits per symbol.

6. Fix GEX model or disable GEX as a decision gate.
- Compute signed dealer gamma or downgrade GEX to log-only.
- Do not treat BTC options as symbol-specific edge for ETH/SOL unless explicitly configured as BTC macro proxy.

7. Improve replay reports.
- The replay report now parses holding MFE/MAE, but still needs:
  - strict per-symbol context association
  - close reason classification
  - fees/commission/net PnL
  - time in trade
  - per-feature win/loss buckets
  - out-of-sample walk-forward split

8. Add an edge dashboard.
- Track expectancy by:
  - symbol

  - strategy
  - HTF alignment
  - CVD divergence state

  - OFI bucket

Bottom line
-----------
The bot is safer and more mechanically reliable than before, but it is not A-grade in strategy edge yet.
The biggest leak is not order placement anymore. It is signal math and exit calibration.

The strongest immediate edge improvement is to fix the CVD divergence/Bayes inversion/capping problem, then recalibrate partials and time exits using the replay report.

Until those are fixed and validated over a larger sample, keep size small. The current sample shows possible directional edge, but not enough net expectancy proof to scale.
