A recorded market view for UK traders.

Crypto derivatives data for traders in the United Kingdom — recorded from public venues, with a source and timestamp on every panel.

UK traders usually follow the London session, but crypto derivatives run around the clock. Quant Desk keeps the recorded view in one place: cross-venue funding, open interest, liquidation clusters, positioning and macro context.

The Financial Conduct Authority restricts retail access to certain crypto derivatives. Quant Desk does not offer execution or brokerage; it records public market data. Check what you are permitted to trade before acting on anything here.

Funding timestamps are shown in UTC; the desk also lets you read the 8-hour settlements in sequence so London-time conversions are straightforward. The same applies to the 24-hour liquidation heat strip.

The free tier covers the daily read. Pro adds order flow, Hyperliquid whale positions, analytics and unlimited alerts.
