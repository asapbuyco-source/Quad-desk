◆ QUANT DESK

Built in Norway, used globally.

Crypto derivatives data for traders in Norway — recorded from public venues, with a source and timestamp on every panel.

Quant Desk is a family-built derivatives terminal based in Norway. The desk records funding, open interest, liquidations, positioning and macro data from public venues, then publishes each panel with its source and the time it was recorded.

Perpetual funding settles on UTC schedules, which means the 08:00 settlement lands at 09:00 or 10:00 in Norway depending on daylight saving. The funding history and accumulated-funding views show the exact settlement timestamps so the local conversion is never a guess.

Norwegian traders typically access offshore derivatives venues, so local rules and tax treatment matter. We publish data, not advice: verify what is permitted for you, and treat every number as informational.

Start with the free desks — prices, funding, positioning, RSI and liquidations — then upgrade only if you need order flow, whale positions or analytics.

Local notes

Currency reference: NOK. Funding settlements display in UTC; convert to local time on your own schedule. Quant Desk does not offer execution, brokerage or personalised advice.
