Recorded derivatives data for German traders.

Crypto derivatives data for traders in Germany — recorded from public venues, with a source and timestamp on every panel.

German traders often compare EU-regulated access with offshore venues. Quant Desk does not route orders; it records public derivatives data so the comparison is based on the same numbers.

Funding and open interest are shown per venue, with UTC settlement times. The 24-hour liquidation strip separates forced longs from forced shorts, which is useful when a European session move spills into the US session.

BaFin rules and tax treatment are your responsibility. The desk publishes market data and methodology, not investment advice.

Free boards cover the core read. Starter adds order flow, whales and depth; Pro adds the full research stack; Institutional adds API access and seats.

Local notes

Currency reference: EUR. Funding settlements display in UTC; convert to local time on your own schedule. Quant Desk does not offer execution, brokerage or personalised advice.
