Ethereum (ETH) derivatives

ETH is the second-deepest perp market and the most sensitive to rotation from BTC. Its leverage data often shows the first cracks in a crowded move.

Open the ETH terminal →

What moves ETH perp data

Funding rate
ETH funding can swing harder than BTC when narratives rotate. Extreme positive funding warns that longs are paying to hold.
Open interest
Watch OI changes alongside ETH/BTC. Rising ETH OI while ETH/BTC falls can signal aggressive shorts piling in.
Long/short ratio
Retail tends to crowd ETH on momentum. Divergence with top-trader positioning is the useful signal.
Taker flow
ETH is heavily used for hedges, so aggressive flow can mean risk transfer rather than directional conviction. Pair it with price response.
Basis
Futures versus spot. A widening ETH basis points to leverage demand building.

ETH as a rotation signal

When BTC stalls and ETH funding turns hot, leverage is often rotating down the risk curve. When ETH OI spikes into a breakout that fails, the unwind tends to be fast because ETH books are thinner than BTC's.
