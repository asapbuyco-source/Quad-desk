About Quant Desk

Quant Desk is a crypto derivatives terminal for traders who care about what actually happened in the market: executed trades, changes in resting liquidity, funding, open interest and macro context — each shown with its source and timestamp.

What we publish

  • Executed trades with taker-side (aggressor) classification, split by spot and perpetual futures.
  • Order-book liquidity events — size that was executed, cancelled or replenished, sequenced by exchange update id.
  • Rolling flow and price impact over 1s / 5s / 15s / 60s windows.
  • Funding and open interest across Binance, OKX and Bybit, plus the Coinbase spot premium.
  • Macro and FX from FRED and the European Central Bank.
  • News tagged by asset with a sentiment score.

Where the data comes from

Market data is read from public exchange endpoints (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase), the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), the ECB via Frankfurter, and Marketaux for news. Nothing on this site is hand-entered; each panel displays its own as-of time.

What we deliberately do not do

We are not a broker and we do not route orders. We do not provide personalised investment advice, and we do not guarantee any result. Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong — verify anything you act on.
