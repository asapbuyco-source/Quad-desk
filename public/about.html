About Quant Desk

Quant Desk is a crypto derivatives terminal built by a family group in Norway. It is owned by Tanue Alain Che, and developed together with his son, who writes the code and runs the data collection.

It started because the useful numbers were scattered across a dozen tabs — and the dashboards that gathered them rarely showed where the numbers came from.

The story

Tanue spent years watching derivatives markets the slow way: comparing funding on one exchange, open interest on another, liquidations somewhere else. The family project began as a private desk to put those views in one place, with a simple rule — every number must carry its source and the time it was recorded.

The desk is also a family affair. Tanue's son builds and maintains the platform, from the collection workers to the charts. The project was inspired by Tanue's wife, whose patience and encouragement turned a personal tool into something the family could share with other traders.

What we believe

  • Recorded, not guessed. If a feed is unavailable, the panel says so.
  • Free core. Prices, funding, positioning, RSI, liquidations and macro stay free.
  • No signals. We publish market data. We do not route orders or promise returns.
  • Built to be checked. The methodology and coverage pages list sources, cadence and limits.

Where the data comes from

Public exchange endpoints (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase), Hyperliquid for whale accounts, FRED for macro series and the European Central Bank for reference FX. Every board prints its own as-of time; nothing is hand-entered.
