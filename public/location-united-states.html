US spot premium, global derivatives.

Crypto derivatives data for traders in the United States — recorded from public venues, with a source and timestamp on every panel.

US spot venues and offshore derivatives often price the same asset differently. Quant Desk records the Coinbase premium alongside global funding and open interest so the gap is visible instead of implied.

Access to crypto derivatives varies by state and venue. Nothing on this site is an offer to trade; it is recorded public data. Confirm eligibility with your venue and local rules.

The desk shows funding in UTC and the Coinbase-versus-Binance premium with its recording time. That makes it usable across US time zones without manually converting sessions.

Free boards include prices, funding, positioning, RSI, liquidations, macro and news. Pro adds order flow, whale positions and analytics.

Local notes

Currency reference: USD. Funding settlements display in UTC; convert to local time on your own schedule. Quant Desk does not offer execution, brokerage or personalised advice.
