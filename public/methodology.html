Methodology

Where the numbers come from, how often they refresh, and what we deliberately do not do.

Rule: every panel prints its source and the time it was recorded. If a source is unavailable, the panel says so instead of showing a guessed value.

Fallbacks and gaps

Cloud egress IPs are sometimes rate-limited by Binance. When that happens the RSI screener and funding heatmap fall back to Bybit linear and label the source accordingly. Long/short and top-trader ratios are Binance-only; when the endpoint is unavailable those panels show no data rather than filling the gap.

The liquidation collector keeps a rolling in-memory buffer. On a cold start it seeds 24 hours of OKX history, so the map is useful immediately but the archive does not extend beyond 24 hours yet.

What we do not do

We do not route orders, run an exchange, or publish trade signals. We do not claim to predict price. Data is informational only and is not investment advice. Read the risk disclaimer.
