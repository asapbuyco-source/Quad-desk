Referrals

If you already trade, signing up to a venue through the desk's link is the simplest way to support it — at no extra cost to you.

How it works

  • Open the terminal and go to Plans, where active partner links are listed.
  • Create or fund an account on that venue through the link.
  • The venue may pay the desk a share of the trading fee. Your fee is unchanged.

Disclosure: partner links are marked as sponsored where they appear. They never change the data, the rankings or the analysis. We do not accept payment to feature a venue in the terminal.

For creators and communities

If you run a trading community and want tracked links or a co-branded view, contact us. Tracking is being set up; until then links are issued manually and reported monthly.
