Glossary

Order flow
The stream of executed trades plus changes to resting orders. It answers "who was aggressive, and what did the book do about it?"
Aggressor (taker) side
The side that crossed the spread. Buy aggressors lift the ask; sell aggressors hit the bid. Price direction alone does not tell you this.
Executed vs cancelled vs replenished
When size leaves a price level, it either traded (executed) or was pulled (cancelled). When it returns, it was replenished. The distinction separates real supply/demand from spoofing.
Funding rate
A periodic payment between longs and shorts on perpetual futures. Positive funding: longs pay shorts (crowded long). Negative funding: shorts pay longs.
Open interest (OI)
Contracts still open, not contracts traded. Rising OI with rising price suggests new longs; falling OI suggests positions closing.
Liquidation
A forced closure when margin runs out. Clusters of liquidations act like fuel for short-term moves.
Price impact / absorption
Price impact is how far price moves per unit of aggressive volume. Heavy buying with little movement implies sellers are absorbing; heavy buying with strong movement implies buyers are making progress.
Coinbase premium
US spot price (Coinbase) versus the offshore reference. A persistent premium suggests US buyers are bidding above global.

Frequently asked questions

What is order flow in crypto trading?

Order flow is the record of executed trades and changes to resting orders. It shows who was aggressive, at what price, and how liquidity was added or removed.

What does aggressor side mean?

The aggressor is the side that crossed the spread. A buy aggressor lifted the ask; a sell aggressor hit the bid. It is not the same as the price ticking up or down.

What is funding rate in perpetual futures?

A periodic payment between longs and shorts that keeps the perpetual price near spot. Positive funding means longs pay shorts, usually when positioning is crowded long.

How is open interest different from volume?

Volume counts contracts traded; open interest counts contracts still open. Rising open interest with rising price suggests new longs, while falling open interest suggests positions closing.

What is price impact and absorption?

Price impact is how far price moves per unit of aggressive volume. Large aggressive buying with little price movement suggests sellers are absorbing the flow.
