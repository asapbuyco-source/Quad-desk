Bitcoin (BTC) derivatives

BTC perpetuals are the deepest crypto market, so their derivatives data is the cleanest signal of how leverage is positioned. Here is what to watch.

What moves BTC perp data

Funding rate
Positive funding means longs pay shorts — a sign positioning is crowded long. Sustained high positive funding often precedes long liquidations.
Open interest
Rising OI with rising price implies new longs; rising OI with falling price implies new shorts. Falling OI means positions are closing.
Long/short ratio
Retail accounts tend to chase. Comparing retail ratios with top-trader positioning is more useful than either alone.
Taker buy/sell
Aggressive market orders. Buy dominance with little price movement suggests sellers are absorbing.
Coinbase premium
US spot demand versus offshore. A persistent premium points to US buyers bidding above the global reference.

Why BTC data leads the market

BTC carries the most open interest and the tightest spreads, so funding, basis and liquidation levels there tend to set the tone for the rest of the market. When BTC funding flips hard negative while price holds, it often marks a short-squeeze setup — the reverse of crowded longs.
