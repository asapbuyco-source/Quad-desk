Solana (SOL) derivatives

SOL is a high-beta perp market: funding and open interest move faster and liquidations hit harder. That makes it a good place to see crowding before it unwinds.

Open the SOL terminal →

What moves SOL perp data

Funding rate
SOL funding spikes are common in momentum runs. Very high positive funding is a warning, not an invitation.
Open interest
Thin books mean OI can build quickly. Fast OI growth into a wick often precedes a sharp squeeze.
Long/short ratio
SOL retail positioning is momentum-driven. When everyone is long into resistance, the downside move is faster.
Taker flow
Aggressive flow plus rising OI means new positions; aggressive flow plus falling OI means closing/absorption.
Liquidations
Liquidation clusters on SOL are wider apart than BTC, so moves can overshoot before finding fuel.

High-beta leverage

Because SOL moves more per unit of flow, the gap between funding and price response is informative. If funding is hot but price stops making progress, longs are paying for a move that is not happening — a classic setup for a flush.
